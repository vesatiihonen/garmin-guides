Morning Activation (Rainy)

25-minute total: 8 min home prep (jump rope, glute bridges, Tib Bar) + 2 min warm-up + 10 min indoor circuit + 3 min cool-down + 2 min walk. For rainy mornings (no field). Same intensity, all indoors.

⏰ Gate: Hamstring Test (Mandatory)

Before starting, do a 1 min easy march in place. Your call:

✓ GREEN Tight but pain-free → proceed with full circuit
△ AMBER Sharp pain or very stiff → skip plyos, just walk and stretch
✗ RED Can't move → rest day, walk only

1 Home Prep (Kitchen or Bedroom)

8–10 min · Right after waking

Tib Bar Activation: 15–20 reps (pull toes toward shins against resistance).

Jump rope: 2 × 60–90 sec (easy bounce, ankles only). Quiet landings.

Glute bridges: 2 × 12 reps (lie on back, knees bent, press heels, lift hips, squeeze glutes at top 1–2 sec).

Hydration: Drink 200 ml water before starting indoor circuit.

Why home: same as outdoor version, prep is always indoors.

2 Warm-Up (Hallway or Living Room)

~2 min

Slow march in place: 1 min, knees lifting gently. Get HR to ~100–110 bpm.

Easy dynamic stretch: 1 min — arm circles, leg swings (forward/back, side-to-side), torso twists.

3 Main Circuit (Indoors)

10 min (2 rounds × 5 min each)

Round 1 + Round 2, same moves (clear space needed ~2m × 2m):

  1. High knees in place: 45 sec (footwork, ankle coordination, HR up)
  2. Jump rope or double-unders: 45 sec (if rope unavailable, do jumping jacks instead)
  3. Single-leg balance on floor: 20 sec per leg (eyes open, hand on wall if needed for safety). Start with eyes open, advance to eyes closed if strong.
  4. March in place or light step-touches: 1 min (active recovery between rounds, keep HR up)

Tip: Same total work as outdoor version, but you control the space and weather. Move with control — explosiveness > volume.

4 Cool-Down (Living Room)

2–3 min
  1. Slow march in place for 1 min, catch your breath.
  2. Light hamstring stretch (fold forward, touch toes, 30 sec). Easy warm hold, no bouncing.
  3. Standing quad stretch (hold one leg up, 15 sec each side).

5 Optional: Light Movement

~2 min

Walk around the house or do 2 min of easy walking (outdoor if rain has stopped). Elevate heart rate slightly to sustain the conditioning.

✓ Session Complete

You've burned ~250–300 kcal and activated every joint for the day. This is pure weight-loss + resilience work, not recovery. If legs feel heavy at the end → that's the signal today's running will feel snappier.

Hydration after: Drink 300–400 ml water in the hour after. Electrolytes if sweaty.

When to choose indoor vs outdoor: If rain is heavy or field is wet, choose this version. Same time, same intensity. The goal is consistent 07:30 activation every weekday — weather doesn't change that.
