Morning Activation Run

20-minute door-to-door session at 07:30 AM (Mon–Fri). Purpose: weight loss (75→70 kg), ankle strength, sleep rhythm.

⏰ Gate: Hamstring Test (Mandatory)

Before field work, do a 1 min easy jog in place at home. Your call:

✓ GREEN Tight but pain-free → proceed with full circuit
△ AMBER Sharp pain or very stiff → skip plyos, just walk
✗ RED Can't jog at all → rest day, walk only

1 Pre-Field (Home)

1–2 min · Right after waking

Ankle Activation: Tib Bar dorsiflexion 15–20 reps (pull toes toward shins against resistance).

Hydration: Drink 200 ml water before heading out.

2 Walk to Field

~3 min

Easy pace. Your eyes open, body waking up.

3 Field Warm-Up

2 min

Easy jog 1 lap around the grass field (~400m). Get HR to ~110–120 bpm.

4 Main Circuit

10 min (2 rounds × 5 min each)

Round 1 + Round 2, same moves:

  1. Jump rope: 1 min continuous (calf + ankle power)
  2. High knees / A-skips: 30 sec (footwork, ankle coordination)
  3. Single-leg hops: 30 sec per leg (60 sec total — balance + power)
  4. Glute bridges: 20 sec (hamstring-safe, butt activation)
  5. Single-leg balance on grass: 20 sec per leg (proprioception — eyes open)
  6. Easy jog around field: 1 min (active recovery between rounds)

Tip: Grass is forgiving on joints. Move fast but controlled — explosiveness > volume.

5 Cool-Down

1–2 min · On the field
  1. Walk a lap, catch your breath.
  2. Light hamstring stretch (fold forward, touch toes, 30 sec).

6 Walk Home

~3–5 min

Easy pace. Elevate heart rate on the way back slightly to sustain the conditioning.

✓ Session Complete

You've burned ~250–300 kcal and activated every joint for the day. This is pure weight-loss + resilience work, not recovery. If legs feel heavy at the end → that's the signal tomorrow's running will feel snappier.

Hydration after: Drink 300–400 ml water in the hour after you get home. Electrolytes if sweaty.

Consistency is the goal: 07:30 AM start (Mon–Fri) = sleep rhythm anchor + 5× week = ~1200 kcal/week burned toward your 75→70 kg goal. Workouts will shift around training, but the activation stays the same.
