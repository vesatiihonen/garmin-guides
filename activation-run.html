Morning Activation Run

25-minute total: 8 min home prep (jump rope, glute bridges, Tib Bar) + 3 min walk + 8 min field circuit + 3 min cool-down + 3 min walk home. Start 07:30 AM (Mon–Fri). Purpose: weight loss (75→70 kg), ankle strength, sleep rhythm.

⏰ Gate: Hamstring Test (Mandatory)

Before field work, do a 1 min easy jog in place at home. Your call:

✓ GREEN Tight but pain-free → proceed with full circuit
△ AMBER Sharp pain or very stiff → skip plyos, just walk
✗ RED Can't jog at all → rest day, walk only

1 Home Prep (Before Field)

8–10 min · Right after waking

Tib Bar Activation: 15–20 reps (pull toes toward shins against resistance).

Jump rope: 2 × 60–90 sec (easy bounce, ankles only). Quiet landings.

Glute bridges: 2 × 12 reps (lie on back, knees bent, press heels, lift hips, squeeze glutes at top 1–2 sec).

Hydration: Drink 200 ml water before heading to field.

Why home: jump rope + bridges are impact-sensitive; dry ground + control beats potentially wet field.

2 Walk to Field

~3 min

Easy pace. Your eyes open, body waking up.

3 Field Warm-Up

2 min

Easy jog 1 lap around the grass field (~400m). Get HR to ~110–120 bpm.

4 Main Circuit (Field)

8 min (2 rounds × 4 min each)

Round 1 + Round 2, same moves:

  1. High knees / A-skips: 30 sec (footwork, ankle coordination)
  2. Single-leg hops: 30 sec per leg (60 sec total — balance + power). Legs stay long, bounce from ankle.
  3. Single-leg balance on grass: 20 sec per leg (eyes open, proprioception).
  4. Easy jog around field: 1 min (active recovery between rounds)

Tip: Grass is forgiving on joints. Move fast but controlled — explosiveness > volume. Dry ground, wet-safe moves only.

5 Cool-Down (Field)

2–3 min
  1. Walk a lap, catch your breath.
  2. Light hamstring stretch (fold forward, touch toes, 30 sec). Easy warm hold, no bouncing.

6 Walk Home

~3–5 min

Easy pace. Elevate heart rate on the way back slightly to sustain the conditioning.

✓ Session Complete

You've burned ~250–300 kcal and activated every joint for the day. This is pure weight-loss + resilience work, not recovery. If legs feel heavy at the end → that's the signal tomorrow's running will feel snappier.

Hydration after: Drink 300–400 ml water in the hour after you get home. Electrolytes if sweaty.

Consistency is the goal: 07:30 AM start (Mon–Fri) = sleep rhythm anchor + 5× week = ~1200 kcal/week burned toward your 75→70 kg goal. Workouts will shift around training, but the activation stays the same.
