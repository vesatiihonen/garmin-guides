💡 Tips
  • Be specific: "HR was 160, felt easy" tells me more than "felt good"
  • Muscle feel: tight, sore, springy, heavy, niggles — all matter
  • Compare: "easier than last time" or "harder than expected" helps me dial in the plan
  • Mistakes: "hit threshold too early" or "started too hard" — I use these to adjust next session
  • Finnish or English — doesn't matter. Write like you're chatting.
