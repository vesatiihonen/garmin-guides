Recovery Core + Mobility (Light)

Date: Sunday, Aug 16, 2026 | Time: 25–30 min | After: 26km long easy run

Purpose: Activation + parasympathetic recovery. NOT new stimulus. Prepare for Monday quality work.

A. Light Core (15 min)

1. Plank Hold

2 sets × 30 sec

  • Forearm plank, body straight (don't sag)
  • Breathe normally (don't hold breath)
  • Rest 30 sec between sets
  • Focus: core engagement, not maximum tension
2. Dead Bug

2 sets × 8 reps (alternating)

  • Lie on back, knees 90°, arms extended up
  • Extend left arm + right leg (80% range, not end-range)
  • Return to center, then right arm + left leg
  • Tempo: 2 sec up, 1 sec hold
  • Purpose: anti-rotation core, no hamstring demand
3. Glute Bridge Hold

3 sets × 20 sec hold

  • Lie on back, feet flat, knees 90°
  • Push hips up, squeeze glutes hard
  • Hold 20 sec (breathe, don't relax)
  • Lower, rest 20 sec
  • Purpose: dorsal chain activation, low CNS cost

B. Mobility (10–15 min)

Warm holds only — NO BOUNCE, just gentle stretch.

1. Hamstring Stretch

2 min total (30 sec per leg)

  • Stand, place foot on bench/chair, fold forward gently
  • Warm hold (no jerking), feel hamstring stretch
  • 30 sec per leg, repeat
2. Calf Stretch

1 min total (30 sec per leg)

  • Step forward with one leg, back heel on ground
  • Feel calf stretch, warm hold 30 sec
  • Swap legs
3. Hip Flexor Stretch

1 min total (30 sec per side)

  • Half-lunge: front leg 90°, back knee down
  • Feel front of hip (flexor) stretch
  • 30 sec per side, warm hold
4. Pigeon Pose (Gluteus)

1–2 min total (1 min per side)

  • From quadruped (hands + knees), bring left knee forward
  • Fold forward gently, feel glute stretch
  • 1 min per side, warm hold, breathe
5. Legs-Up-the-Wall (Parasympathetic)

2–3 min

  • Lie on back, legs up wall, hips close to base
  • Slide hips away from wall until legs are extended up
  • Relax, breathe deeply (5 sec in, 7 sec out)
  • Purpose: parasympathetic activation = recovery signal to nervous system
Total Duration: 25–30 min

Post-Session Recovery

  • Hydrate: 300–500 ml water
  • Nutrition: Light snack if hungry (carbs + protein, no heavy fat before sleep)
  • Sleep: Prioritize 8+ hours tonight — recovery consolidates the 26km stimulus
Key Mindset: This is NOT a training session. This is active recovery — activation + parasympathetic signal. Tomorrow (Monday) quality work gates on recovery tonight.
