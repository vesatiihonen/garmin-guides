Drills

5–8 min · every session
01

Skipping rope

2 × 60–90 s · easy bounce · ankles only

  • Balls of the feet, heels never touch down.
  • Quiet landings — barely hear yourself.
  • Tight space (travel fallback): low ceiling → do the "invisible rope" (same footwork, no rope); keep bounces low.

✕ squatting and jumping high off the knees

on YouTube ↗
02

Pogos / ankle hops

2 × 15 hops · minimal ground time

  • Legs stay long — bounce from the ankle, not the knee.
  • Off the ground fast, like hot pavement.
  • Tight space (travel fallback): keep them low + soft (small, quick, quiet).

✕ bending the knees to absorb the landing

on YouTube ↗
03

A-skips

2 × 20 m · rhythmic, light

  • Drive knee to thigh-horizontal, toe pulled up.
  • Strike the ground under the hip, not out front.
  • Tight space (travel fallback): do them on the spot — drive the knee up, cycle the foot down under you, stay in place.

✕ leaning back to get the knee higher

on YouTube ↗
04

High-knee march

2 × 20 m · slow, controlled

  • Walk, don't hop — one foot always down.
  • Tall spine, ribs stacked over hips.
  • Tight space (travel fallback): march tall in place — soft, quiet steps, no forward travel needed.

✕ rushing it into a jog

Core

8–12 min · every session
05

Front plank

3 × 30–45 s · one straight line

  • Elbows under shoulders; squeeze glutes.
  • Breathe normally — if you can't, shorten it.

✕ hips sagging (dashed) or piked up high

06

Side plank

2 × 20–30 s each side

  • Elbow under shoulder; push the bottom hip up.
  • Top arm straight up, chest open.

✕ bottom hip drifting toward the ground

07

Dead bug

2 × 8–10 each side · slow

  • Extend opposite arm + leg out low, then return.
  • Exhale as you extend; low back stays flat.

✕ low back arching off the floor

08

Bird dog

2 × 8–10 each side · pause 2 s

  • Reach opposite arm + leg to horizontal, no higher.
  • Hips square — glass of water on your back.

✕ twisting the hips open to lift the leg

Glute bridge

2 × 12–15 · or single-leg / banded

  • Heels close to glutes; drive through the heels.
  • Squeeze glutes at the top, hold 2 s.

Clamshells

2 × 12 each side · band above knees

  • Feet together, open the top knee like a clam.
  • Keep hips stacked — don't roll back.

12

Copenhagen plank

2 × 15–20 s each side · short lever

  • Top knee on the bench (or ab/back bench).
  • Squeeze the surface, lift hips to a straight line.
  • Build +5 s a week — never rush this one.

Posterior chain

hamstring + calf · priority now

Your hamstring flags on the downhills — this is the tissue that fixes that. Eccentric (slow-lower) strength is what lets it take the descent. Pain-free range only; add these after the core.

13

Single-leg RDL

2 × 8–10 each leg · slow lower

  • Hinge at the hip, back flat; feel the hamstring stretch.
  • Push the free heel to the wall behind you; hips stay square.
  • Home: hold a DB / KB for load once steady — keep it light today (moderate).

✕ rounding the back or twisting the hip open

on YouTube ↗
14

Hamstring curl

2 × 12–15 each leg · control the lower

  • Band on the ankle: curl the heel toward your glute.
  • The slow lower is the point — that's what protects downhills.
  • No band? Towel-slider bridge curls on a smooth floor.

✕ swinging the whole leg from the hip

on YouTube ↗
15

Calf raise

3 × 15–20 · full range, slow down

  • Up on the toes, pause, lower slowly (below level off a step).
  • Do a set knee-straight and a set knee-bent (soleus) — both feed running.
  • Home: off the step for full range; single-leg, then hold a DB for load.

✕ bouncing at the bottom for reps

on YouTube ↗

Order: drills first while the legs are fresh, then core, the banded hip work, and the posterior chain last. 6×/week, one day off.

Home: the posterior chain is your priority weakness — this is where the garage load (DB/KB, step, bench) earns its keep. Add load gradually week to week; back it off on amber/low-recovery days.

Safety: adductors strain easily — any sharp groin or Achilles pain means stop the session, not push through it.
