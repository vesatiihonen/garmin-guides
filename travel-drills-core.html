Drills

6 min · neuromuscular activation · do first, fresh legs
★
Critical for power

Squat jumps (double-leg explosive)

3 × 6 reps · maximum height · 30 sec rest

  • Why: Research-backed best exercise for explosive power (Cooper test = power + aerobic).
  • Feet hip-width, bend knees 45°, explode up as high as possible.
  • Land soft, absorb with bent knees, immediately into next rep.
  • Quality over quantity: max height each jump, not speed.

✕ Landing stiff-legged (kills power, injures knees)

01
Ankle power

Skipping rope

2 × 60–90 s · easy bounce · ankles only

  • Balls of feet, heels never touch. Quiet landings.
  • Tight space: invisible rope (same footwork, no rope).
02
Ankle stiffness / power

Pogos / ankle hops

2 × 15 hops · minimal ground time

  • Bounce from ankles, not knees. Off the ground fast.
  • Tight space: low, soft bounces.
03
Running form

A-skips

2 × 20 m · rhythmic, light

  • Drive knee to thigh-horizontal, toe up. Strike under hip.
  • Tight space: on-the-spot (knee drive, cycle down).
04
Running form

B-skips (add a bound)

2 × 20 m · rhythm + bound

  • A-skip but add a small bound after each knee drive.
  • Knee → bound → knee → bound rhythm.
  • Purpose: power transition for the Cooper sprint finish.
05
Running form

High-knee march

2 × 20 m · slow, tall

  • Walk, don't hop. Tall spine, knees drive high.
  • Tight space: march in place, tall, quiet.

Core

10 min · stability (anti-rotation, anti-extension) · every session
★
Critical stability

Front plank

3 × 40–50 s · one straight line · 30 sec rest

  • Elbows under shoulders, squeeze glutes hard.
  • Breathe steadily — if you can't, shorter hold.

✕ Hips sagging or piked high

★
Anti-rotation

Side plank

2 × 25–35 s each side · 30 sec rest

  • Elbow under shoulder, push bottom hip up.
  • Top arm straight up, chest open.
07
Anti-extension

Dead bug

2 × 8 each side · slow, 2 sec each direction

  • Extend opposite arm + leg out low, return.
  • Exhale as you extend, low back flat to floor.
08
Hip stability

Bird dog

2 × 8 per side · pause 1 sec at extension

  • Hands and knees, extend opposite arm + leg in line.
  • Don't rotate torso. Hips and shoulders level.
06
Hip extension (glute focus)

Glute bridge

2 × 12 reps · squeeze top 2 sec

  • Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat. Press heels, lift hips.
  • Squeeze glutes hard at the top (not lower back).

Posterior Chain (Hamstring/Glute/Calf)

12 min · eccentric strength · priority focus
★
Critical hamstring / power

Single-leg RDL (Romanian Deadlift)

3 × 6 per leg · light DB/KB · 2 sec eccentric · 30 sec rest

  • Stand on one leg, hinge at hips (close-car-door motion).
  • Eccentric focus: lower 2 sec, feel hamstring stretch.
  • Stop at your range (don't force floor touch).
  • Drive hips forward to return. Controlled, not fast.

✕ Rounding the back (defeats purpose)

★
Critical calf power (ski push)

Calf raise (step or floor)

3 × 12 · 1 sec squeeze top · 30 sec rest

  • Step: heel off step, rise onto toes.
  • Floor: rise onto balls of feet, squeeze calves hard.
  • Ski push power comes from calf. Make it count.
09
Hamstring endurance

Hamstring curl (band or towel on smooth floor)

2 × 10 · 1 sec squeeze · 30 sec rest

  • Band: loop around foot, lie on back, curl heel toward glute against band.
  • Towel: lie on floor, heels on towel, curl towel toward glute by bending knees.
  • Squeeze hamstring at the top. Slower = harder.

Ankle Strength (Slip Resilience)

6 min · dorsiflexion + calf symmetry · do with posterior chain
★
Critical slip prevention

Tib Bar dorsiflexion (Anterior tibialis)

2 × 12–15 · 1 sec squeeze top

  • Sit in chair, feet flat. Use Tib Bar or resistance band looped around balls of feet.
  • Pull toes toward shins against resistance. Feel front of shin.
  • Why: Anterior tibialis prevents foot inversion (ankle roll). Stronger tib = slip resilience.
10
Symmetry (calf paired)

Calf raise (for symmetry, if not done above)

2 × 12 · same tempo as Tib Bar

  • Pair with Tib Bar for ankle balance: pull toes up (shin) ↔ rise onto toes (calf).
  • Both work different planes. Together = ankle resilience.
11
Proprioception

Single-leg balance (balance board or BOSU)

2 × 30 sec per leg · eyes open · 30 sec rest

  • Stand on one leg on unstable surface (balance board, BOSU, folded towel).
  • Quiet feet, engage ankle stabilizers (whole ankle works, not just foot).
  • Purpose: trains the reflexes that catch a slip before it happens.

Mobility + Stretch

3–5 min · cool-down · do if time + energy

Hamstring stretch (static)

2 × 30 sec per leg

  • Lie on back, pull one knee toward chest (or use a towel around foot).
  • Breathe. Gentle, warm hold (not aggressive).

Calf stretch (static)

2 × 30 sec per leg

  • Face a wall, step back with one leg, keep heel down.
  • Lean forward gently until you feel the stretch.

Hip / glute stretch (pigeon pose)

2 × 30 sec per side

  • Sit on floor, one leg bent in front, other extended behind.
  • Fold forward gently over the bent leg.

Time crunch? If you're running short, complete in this order: (1) Squat jumps (2) RDL (3) Calf raises (4) Tib Bar (5) Core planks (6) Remaining drills. The red ★ moves drive Cooper fitness most.
